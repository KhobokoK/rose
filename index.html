🌹 Our Journey 🌹

2021

That’s when I first saw you at Leruo,,, during our supplementary classes. I didn’t know then that “ke tla o rata hakana” . You made me see Maths differently because your presence made every moment brighter.

2022

I carried a quiet promise in my heart,, that one day, re tla kopana hape at tertiary. Even though distance and time stood between us, my hope never faded.

2024

That dream came true. We met again and this time, not just as classmates, but as partners in life. We studied, we laughed, we fought, and we built something real. O ile oa nthuta hore lerato ha se mantsoe feela, empa ke ketso you taught me that love is action.

2025

Now here we are .. your graduation year. From Quthing’s dusty fields to walking proudly in your gown on 7 November. Ke motlotlo ka wena haholo, lerato la ka . You’ve become the woman I always knew you could be strong, graceful, and unstoppable. ❤️
